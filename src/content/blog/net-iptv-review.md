## Net IPTV review: the short answer

Net IPTV suits households that want a large live-TV and on-demand library on the screens they already own, with month-to-month flexibility and no contract. It suits you less if you only need one or two specific channels, or if your home connection can't hold a steady 15 Mbps for Full HD.

What we think holds up well: the size of the library, activation that usually takes under five minutes, a published refund policy, payments with buyer protection, and support that answers on WhatsApp and live chat around the clock. What you should weigh: channel availability can differ when you travel, picture quality depends on your own connection, and the monthly plan allows only one stream at a time.

The rest of this review explains each of those points in detail, so you can decide on facts rather than adjectives.

## How Net IPTV handles payment, contracts and refunds

Net IPTV is prepaid with no contract. You pay for the term you choose, plans don't renew automatically unless you opt in at checkout, and the terms give a 7-day money-back guarantee from first activation, paid back to the original payment method.

**Payment.** Major debit and credit cards, PayPal, and selected cryptocurrencies. Card and PayPal payments run through a payment provider, so we never see or store your full card number, and both come with the buyer protection of your card issuer or PayPal. If protection matters to you, and with any online subscription it should, pay by card or PayPal.

**Renewal.** Subscriptions end at the close of their term unless you opted into auto-renewal at checkout. If you did, you can switch it off from your account or by messaging support, and the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current term.

**Refunds.** The [refund section of our terms](/terms#refunds) gives a 7-day money-back guarantee from the date of first activation. Contact support within that window and the refund is processed to the original payment method, typically within five business days. It covers the first subscription period, not renewals, and it doesn't apply to accounts used in breach of the acceptable-use rules, such as publicly shared credentials.

**Your statutory rights.** The guarantee is a promise in our terms, separate from consumer law. If you live in the EU, online purchases normally come with a [14-day right of withdrawal](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm), but for digital content and services that right can end once the service starts, if you expressly agreed to that at checkout. That's why we state our own 7-day guarantee in writing rather than relying on the general rule.

## The limitations we want you to know about

No IPTV service is perfect, and a review that lists no weaknesses isn't a review. These are the limitations of Net IPTV worth knowing before you pay, and what you can do about each one. None of them is unusual for IPTV, but you should weigh them against how and where you watch.

1. **Your connection sets the ceiling.** We recommend about 8 Mbps per stream for HD, 15 Mbps for Full HD and 25 Mbps for stable 4K. On weak Wi-Fi the picture drops quality to keep playing. A network cable or 5 GHz Wi-Fi fixes most cases, but we can't fix a slow line.
2. **Line-ups can differ abroad.** A small number of channels are geo-restricted by the broadcaster rather than by us, so what you see while travelling can differ from what you see at home.
3. **Channels occasionally drop.** Most outages are upstream at the source and come back within minutes. Servers fail over automatically, and backup sources are kept for major channels, but a channel can still be unavailable for a short time. Report it on live chat with the channel name.
4. **The monthly plan is single-screen.** One simultaneous connection is fine for one person; families should look at the 3, 6 or 12-month plans.
5. **Player apps are third-party.** Net IPTV runs through player apps such as IBO Player, Smart IPTV or VLC. Some of these charge their own small fee for premium features, separate from your subscription.
6. **Smart TV activation goes through support.** On Samsung and LG TVs and on phones, you send support your device's MAC address rather than typing a password. Support answers around the clock, but it's one extra step compared with a simple login.
7. **You're responsible for local rules.** The law on streaming differs between countries. Check what applies where you live and watch, especially if you're outside your home country.

## Is Net IPTV legit? How to judge any IPTV provider

A legitimate IPTV provider can be checked: it publishes its prices, terms and refund policy, takes payments with buyer protection, lets you test before you buy, and answers questions before you're a customer. Here's how Net IPTV measures up against that standard, and how to apply it to any service.

- **Published terms and policies.** Net IPTV publishes its [terms of service](/terms), privacy policy and refund rules. Read them; a provider without them gives you nothing to hold it to.
- **Payment protection.** Card and PayPal payments are accepted. Be wary of any provider that only takes cryptocurrency or gift cards.
- **A trial before payment.** Available without card details, as described above.
- **Plausible promises.** Treat any service that promises "every channel in the world" for a couple of euros with suspicion, and judge us by what you can see during the trial rather than by a number on a page.
- **Reachable support.** WhatsApp and live chat around the clock. Ask a pre-sales question and see how quickly, and how specifically, you get an answer.
